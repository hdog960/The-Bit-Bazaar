You inherited a nerdy figurine shop. Unfortunately, there is discarded packaging, scattered rubbish and old stains all over the shop. The shelves are dusty and cobwebbed, and nearly all its collection is scattered across the floor.

3,000UNIQUE FIGURES300COMPLETE SETS40SHELF UNITS

START BY CLEANING! Aim at dusty shelves, loose paper, cardboard boxes or floor stains with EMPTY HANDS. Clean shelves in three stages; gather paper, flatten and remove boxes, and scrub floors until they shine. Restored shelves accept figures, and some messes conceal lost miniatures. Find figurines numbered 1–10 in each collection. Aim the center reticle at one of the ten shelf slots to place a figure anywhere in its matching row. Aim at a placed figure to lift it, or swap with a held figure. A set only counts as complete when it reads 1, 2, 3 … 10 from left to right.

iPhone: aim your reticle at a dusty shelf, floor litter, boxes or stains and tap CLEAN with EMPTY HANDS. PUT DOWN frees your hands. Aim to PICK UP, LIFT, SWAP and PLACE figurines. Use ☆ PIN A HUNT in the world to see any pinned set's collecting checklist without opening the Almanac. Desktop: E interact, F inspect, M map, WASD move.
