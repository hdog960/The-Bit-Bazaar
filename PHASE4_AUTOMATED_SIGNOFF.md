## Boundaries
This establishes sufficient **automated** QA to proceed, not proof that every physical iPhone model, native iOS Files destination, Home Screen lifecycle and thermal state are perfect. Those limitations are documented, but **none requires player action or approval before further game development**. An actual user bug report, if volunteered, may guide additional fixes.

**Creative roadmap unchanged:** Phase 5 unlocks permanently at 150 distinct collections perfected at least once; optional glasses must show COWARD and may guide only already-started sets. Phase 6 follows Phase 5. Player progress and save keys are preserved.
