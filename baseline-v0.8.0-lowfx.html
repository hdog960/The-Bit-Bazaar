You inherited a nerdy figurine shop. Unfortunately, the shelves are covered in dust and cobwebs, and nearly all its collection is scattered across the floor.

START BY CLEANING! Aim at a dirty shelf with empty hands: wipe its dust, remove its cobwebs, then clear old packaging. Restored shelves accept figures, and some messes conceal lost miniatures. Find figurines numbered 1–10 in each collection. Aim the center reticle at one of the ten shelf slots to place a figure anywhere in its matching row. Aim at a placed figure to lift it, or swap with a held figure. A set only counts as complete when it reads 1, 2, 3 … 10 from left to right.

iPhone: aim at a dusty shelf and tap CLEAN with empty hands, or aim at a figure to PICK UP, LIFT or SWAP. PUT DOWN frees your hands. LOOK inspects figures. Desktop: E clean/interact, F inspect, WASD move.
